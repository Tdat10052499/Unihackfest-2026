**In one line:** N.E.D is a stablecoin wallet whose differentiator is **Shared Money**: money several people put together (Rotating Fund, Milestone Lock, Group Goal) is held in one Solana program under rules they agreed on, not by a middleman. The first customer segment (Vietnamese workers abroad, or freelancers with foreign clients) is chosen from survey data on 6 Oct 2026.

**Status:** proposal; not yet reviewed by a mentor, the Compliance Lead (`../05-legal/compliance-lead-tasks.md`) or a lawyer. Labels: [Sourced] / [Inference] / [Assumption] / [Unverified]. Product wording follows the terminology table in section 11.3.
