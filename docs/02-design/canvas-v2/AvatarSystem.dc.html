User avatars

Every new N.E.D account gets an avatar the moment its profile is created. Nobody uploads a photo: the avatar is drawn from the wallet address, so it looks the same on every phone and needs no server.

How it is made
  1. Seed = wallet address, not the username, so changing @username never changes the avatar.
  2. A short hash of the seed picks 1 of 8 colours, 1 of 6 patterns, a rotation and an accent: 1,344 combinations.
  3. Drawn as a small SVG in the app. No upload, no photo, no personal data, nothing stored.
  4. Colours come from the design system tints and strong inks, so every avatar sits calmly on white cards.

Not an identity check
Two accounts can get similar avatars, and anyone can copy a picture. Screens must never say "verified" because of an avatar. Before locking money, people check the @username and the on-chain facts.
When it appears
On Create profile, the avatar is shown as soon as the wallet is ready, with the line "Made from your wallet address. It stays the same on every device." It then appears in the Home header, Settings, contract rows, the party row on contract detail, and the freelancer search result.
