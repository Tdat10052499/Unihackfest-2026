Design system

Motion and surfaces

No outlines around components. Depth comes from tone and a soft shadow. Motion is short and calm, uses only opacity and transform, and never blocks a tap.

Rules that keep it smooth

  1. Animate only opacity and transform. Never width, height, top or left.
  2. Nothing longer than 400 ms. Exits run at about 70% of the enter time.
  3. Stagger at most 5 items, 40 ms apart. Long lists animate the first screen only.
  4. Money amounts never count up or bounce. They appear in place, already final.
  5. A tap always works during a transition; nothing waits for an animation to end.
  6. Respect “Reduce motion”: everything becomes an instant change.
