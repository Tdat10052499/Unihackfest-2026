Write the brief. Deliver the work. Get it released.

The Workspace is the computer side of your N.E.D Wallet. Clients write the brief and lock money per milestone. Freelancers submit their work before the deadline. Money moves only from a wallet, when its owner confirms.

Use the same Google account as on your phone.
Client

Write the brief

Scope, milestones and what counts as done. Its fingerprint is saved on-chain, so neither side can change it later.

Freelancer

Submit your work

Links and files from your computer, before the deadline. The chain clock records when you submitted.

Client

Review and release

Check the delivery against the brief, then release. If you do nothing by the review deadline, it releases on its own.
