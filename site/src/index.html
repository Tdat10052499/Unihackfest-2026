How it works

  1. 1 Client

    Write the brief

    Scope, milestones and what counts as done. Its fingerprint is saved on-chain, so neither side can change it later.

  2. 2 Both

    Accept and lock

    The freelancer accepts and chooses where earnings go. The client locks USDC per milestone in a vault that only the program controls.

  3. 3 Both

    Submit, review, release

    The chain clock records each submission. The client approves, or the milestone is released when the review time ends.

The 2-minute judge path

Two people, two screens: Mia, a client in Singapore, and Vinh, a freelancer in Vietnam.

  1. 0:00Mia creates “Landing page design” with 2 milestones of 10 USDC each and sends Vinh the invite link.
  2. 0:20Vinh opens it on his phone, reads the brief and accepts with “VND to my bank account”.
  3. 0:40Mia locks 20 devnet USDC. Vinh sees “≈ 520,000 VND locked (estimate)”.
  4. 1:00Vinh submits milestone 1; Mia approves. Status: “Released to payout partner · VND payout simulated in this demo”.
  5. 1:30A second contract whose review time has ended: anyone presses Release.
  6. 1:45Solana Explorer: the vault is owned by the program, not by N.E.D.

Disclosures

  • Devnet, test money only. Network fees use test SOL.
  • N.E.D holds no funds, converts nothing and charges no fee in the pilot. The money sits in a program vault.
  • The payout partner is simulated in the demo (candidate payout partners: Due, Nium). In the Vietnam path, the freelancer relies on the payout partner after release.
  • No KYC yet. The program is not audited. Phone numbers are not OTP-verified, and the phone hash can be brute-forced.
  • Circle can freeze USDC addresses.
  • Not legal, tax or financial advice.
